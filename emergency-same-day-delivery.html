Frequently Asked

Questions, answered straight.

How fast can you actually get something delivered?
It depends on distance and when you call. Regional same-day is often possible even on short notice, tell us your real deadline and we'll tell you straight whether we can hit it.
Do you charge more for rush jobs?
Yes, urgent jobs cost more because they block out a driver's whole day on short notice. We'll always tell you the price upfront before you commit.
Can I reach the driver directly during an emergency delivery?
Yes. Urgent jobs get direct contact with whoever's actually driving, not a dispatch queue.
What counts as an emergency delivery?
Anything with a real, hard deadline today, medication, documents, parts, anything legal that genuinely can't wait. Tell us the situation and we'll tell you honestly if we're the right fit.
What if you can't make the deadline?
We'll tell you immediately, before you commit, not after. We'd rather say no upfront than take a job we can't deliver on.
