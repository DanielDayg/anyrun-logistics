Found a car three states away and the seller wants a deposit before you've laid eyes on it? We go look at it for you. We check that it matches the listing, take photos and video, note anything off, and give you a straight answer before you commit to anything.

What Actually Matters Here

What moves the price on a car inspection.

What We Check

Exterior condition, interior condition, mileage on the odometer versus the listing, and anything visibly wrong.

Photos & Video

You'll get real photos and video from the actual vehicle, not the ones in the ad.

Distance To The Seller

How far we have to drive to reach the car is the biggest factor in price.

Not A Certified Mechanic

We're your eyes on the ground, not a shop. For a high-value purchase, we can meet a mobile mechanic there if you arrange one.

Good to Know

Good To Know

We give you an honest, non-professional look at the vehicle, not a certified mechanical inspection. We can't guarantee what's under the hood is sound, only what we can see, hear, and check in person before you decide.

Frequently Asked

Questions, answered straight.

Are you mechanics?
No. We're a second set of eyes confirming the car is real, matches the listing, and doesn't have anything obviously wrong. For engine and mechanical concerns, we can meet a mobile mechanic there if you book one.
Can you test drive it?
If the seller allows it, yes. Tell us what you want checked while we're driving it.
What if the car isn't what was advertised?
We'll tell you exactly what's different, mileage, condition, missing features, so you can decide before any money moves.
Can you meet the seller somewhere neutral instead of their house?
Yes, if that's what you and the seller agree on. Just tell us where.
How fast can you get there?
Depends on distance and our schedule. Tell us your timeline when you request a quote and we'll tell you honestly if we can make it work.
