Legal & Regulatory Note

AnyRun handles in-person errands and tasks, not legal representation, notarization, or formal business authority. If your errand requires a licensed professional (attorney, notary, registered agent), tell us and we’ll be upfront about whether it’s something we can run.

Frequently Asked

Business Errands questions, answered straight.

What kind of errands do you handle?
Pickups and drop-offs, waiting in line somewhere, attending something in person on your behalf, picking up paperwork or a physical item that has to be collected by a specific person. If it’s legal and it involves showing up somewhere, ask.
Can you sign for something on my behalf?
In some cases, yes, but it depends on what’s being signed and what authorization is required. We’ll tell you exactly what documentation we need from you before we agree to the job.
Is this the same as a personal assistant service?
It’s narrower: one-off, in-person tasks rather than ongoing assistant work. If you need something recurring, tell us, we can talk about what that looks like.
Can you represent me at a business meeting?
We can attend and relay information, take notes, or handle a specific defined task on your behalf. We’re not a substitute for legal representation or formal business authority, so be clear with us about what “representing you” needs to mean for your situation.
How do I know the errand was actually completed?
You’ll get confirmation, a photo, a timestamp, or a call, depending on the job, the same way you would for any delivery.
