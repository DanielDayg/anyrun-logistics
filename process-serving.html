Good to Know

Important, Read Before You Hire Us

We are not a certified or licensed process-serving company, a notary, or an attorney. In most states, any adult who is not a party to your case can legally serve civil documents, and that's the capacity we work in. Rules vary by state and by court, and some documents or situations require a sheriff, constable, or specially licensed server. You are responsible for confirming your state and court's exact requirements before hiring us, ideally with an attorney if the matter is time-sensitive. We'll ask basic questions about your case upfront and tell you plainly if it's outside what we're able to do.

Frequently Asked

Questions, answered straight.

Are you licensed process servers?
No. In most states, any adult who isn't a party to the case can legally serve civil documents without a special license, and that's the basis we work on. If your state or court specifically requires a sheriff, constable, or certified server, tell us and we'll say upfront whether we're a fit.
Will this hold up in court?
We can't guarantee legal outcomes, that's between you and your court. We'll complete a signed proof of service for you to file, but confirming your court accepts this method of service is your responsibility, ideally checked with an attorney before a deadline.
What documents can you serve?
Divorce papers, subpoenas, eviction notices, small claims paperwork, and similar civil documents where your state permits non-party service. Tell us what it is and we'll tell you honestly if it's something we can do.
How do you keep costs low flying nationwide?
We book through discounted national airline arrangements and plan routes efficiently, so a cross-country serve typically costs less than a local process-serving company charging by the mile.
What if the person won't take the documents?
Tell us your state's rules for that situation if you know them. We'll follow what's legally required for your state and document exactly what happened either way.
