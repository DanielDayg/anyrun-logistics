Frequently Asked

Questions, answered straight.

Can you bring flowers or a gift along with the message?
Yes, tell us what to pick up and where, or send us something to deliver, and we'll bring it with the note.
What if she's not home when you get there?
Tell us your backup plan ahead of time, leave it with someone, try again later, or hold onto it. We won't just leave it on a doorstep unless you say to.
Do you read the message out loud or just hand it over?
Whatever you want. Plenty of people want it read exactly as written. Others just want it handed over quietly.
Will you judge me for this?
No. We've delivered stranger things than a love note.
Can I get confirmation it was delivered?
Yes, we'll let you know once it's done, and can send a photo of the handoff if that's something you want.
