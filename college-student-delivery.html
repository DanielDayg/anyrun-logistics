Frequently Asked

Questions, answered straight.

Can you deliver to a specific dorm room or mail room?
Yes, tell us the building, room, and any check-in process the campus requires and we'll work around it.
Can you drive my student's car to or from school?
Yes, that's handled as a standard vehicle transport job, see our vehicle transport page for how that's priced, or just tell us it's a college move when you request a quote.
How fast can you get something there before an exam or deadline?
Tell us the real deadline and we'll tell you honestly whether it's doable. Same-day and next-day are often possible for regional routes.
Can this be a recurring thing during the school year?
Yes, some families book us multiple times a semester. Mention that when you reach out and we can talk about what makes sense.
What if my student isn't there to receive it?
Tell us the backup plan, leaving it with a roommate, an RA, or a campus mail room, and we'll follow it.
