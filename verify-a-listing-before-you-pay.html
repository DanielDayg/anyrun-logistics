Frequently Asked

Questions, answered straight.

What exactly will you check?
That the address is real, the place matches the listing photos, and that whoever's on site can answer basic questions. Tell us anything specific you're worried about and we'll ask.
Can you do this same day?
Often, depending on distance and our schedule. Tell us your timeline when you request a quote.
What if no one's there when we arrive?
We'll tell you that plainly. An empty lot or a locked unit with nobody around is itself useful information before you send money.
Will you confront someone if it looks like a scam?
No, we're not there to accuse anyone. We report back what we saw, and you decide what to do with it.
What do I get back from you?
Photos, notes on what we found, and an honest read on whether it matched the listing.
