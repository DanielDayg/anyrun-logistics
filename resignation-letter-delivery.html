Frequently Asked

Questions, answered straight.

Do you read the letter or just hand it over?
Just hand it over, unless you specifically ask us to say something along with it.
Can you time it for right when my shift starts or ends?
Yes, tell us the exact time and we'll plan to be there.
What if they want to talk to me right there?
We'll let them know you're not available and the letter speaks for itself. We won't field questions on your behalf.
Is this legally the same as quitting in person?
That depends on your employer's policies, we're not able to give legal advice on that. We simply deliver the document.
Can you also grab my belongings from my desk while you're there?
Often, yes. Tell us what to collect and we'll coordinate it as part of the same trip.
