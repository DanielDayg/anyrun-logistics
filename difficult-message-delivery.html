Frequently Asked

Questions, answered straight.

Is this a real service people actually use?
Yes. Sometimes a conversation needs to happen in person and neither person can make the trip. We take these seriously and handle them with real discretion, not as a novelty.
Will you actually say what I tell you to say?
Yes, word for word if that's what you want, or in our own words if you'd rather it come across more naturally. Tell us which you prefer.
What if they react badly?
We handle it calmly and professionally, and we'll tell you honestly how it went. We're there to deliver the message, not to argue on your behalf.
Is this legal?
Yes, we're delivering a personal message, not doing anything deceptive or harmful. If your situation involves formal legal service, like a restraining order or legal notice, that requires a licensed process server, not us.
How private is this?
Completely. We don't discuss your situation with anyone outside AnyRun Logistics.
