Frequently Asked

Questions, answered straight.

When does it actually make sense to fly instead of drive?
When the distance is far enough and the deadline is tight enough that driving genuinely can't make it. We'll tell you honestly if driving is actually faster or cheaper for your situation, we're not trying to upsell you on flying.
What kind of jobs justify flying?
Urgent documents, a meeting you can't miss, a delivery with a hard deadline, anything where being physically present matters and time is the real constraint.
Is this expensive?
More than driving, yes, flights and short-notice bookings cost real money. We'll give you the full number before you commit to anything.
Can you fly same-day?
Often, depending on flight availability. Tell us your deadline and we'll check what's actually possible.
What happens once you land?
Whatever the job requires, handing something over, attending a meeting, picking something up. We'll confirm the exact plan with you before booking travel.
