Frequently Asked

Questions, answered straight.

How far will you drive something perishable?
Depends on the item and the time window. Tell us both when you request a quote and we'll tell you honestly if it's doable.
Do you have refrigeration in the vehicle?
Not standard, but for temperature-sensitive items we can plan coolers or ice packs. Tell us what it needs.
What if it's a wedding cake or something fragile?
Tell us the size, tiers, and how it's boxed. We'll plan the drive around keeping it level and secure.
Can you deliver on a tight time window, like a specific hour?
Yes, that's the point of driving it instead of shipping it. Tell us the exact window.
What happens if something goes wrong in transit?
We'll tell you immediately and figure out next steps together. We take care in transit, but we're upfront if something happens.
