Frequently Asked

Questions, answered straight.

Do I need to already know exactly where it is?
It helps, but doesn't have to be exact. Tell us what you know, the venue, the rideshare trip, the general area, and we'll work from there.
Can you deal with rideshare lost and found for me?
Yes, tell us the details of the trip and what you lost, and we'll coordinate the pickup once it's located.
What if I need to prove it's mine?
Tell us ahead of time if a place requires ID or proof of ownership and we'll plan around it, sometimes that means you'll need to call ahead yourself first.
Can you ship it instead of driving it?
Depending on distance and the item, yes. Tell us what works better for your timeline.
What if the place already gave it away or threw it out?
We'll tell you immediately so you're not left wondering. That's a real risk with lost and found, the sooner you request the pickup, the better the odds.
