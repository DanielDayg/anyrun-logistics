Frequently Asked

Questions, answered straight.

Will you actually read the message out loud if I want that?
Yes, if that's what you want, tell us exactly what to say and how to say it. Some messages land better spoken than handed over on paper.
What if the person refuses to accept it or isn't there?
We'll tell you honestly what happened, whether that's a refusal, a no-show, or someone who wasn't home. We can also try a second attempt if you want.
Is this legal?
Yes. We're not serving legal papers or doing anything deceptive, we're delivering a personal message the way a trusted friend would if they lived closer. If your situation actually involves legal service of process, tell us and we'll point you to a licensed process server instead.
Can you deliver something alongside the message, like a gift?
Yes, see our gift delivery page, or just tell us in the job details and we'll bundle it into the same trip.
How private is this?
Completely. We don't discuss your job with anyone outside AnyRun Logistics, and we don't ask why, just what you need delivered and to whom.
