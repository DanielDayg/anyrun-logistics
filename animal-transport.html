How Pricing Works

What actually moves the price on animal transport.

Species & Size

A cat in a carrier and a full-size dog need different space and handling. Livestock is priced separately based on the animal and trailer or vehicle needs.

Distance & Trip Length

Longer trips need planned rest, water, and temperature stops built into the route and the price.

Temperament & Special Needs

Anxious animals, medical needs, or medication schedules change how a trip is planned. Tell us upfront so nothing gets missed.

Documentation

Interstate animal movement can require health certificates or paperwork depending on the species and states involved. We’ll flag what you need before the trip, not after.

Good to Know

Legal & Regulatory Note

Interstate transport of livestock and some animals can require health certificates and state-specific paperwork. We’ll flag what applies to your job, but confirming with your vet and the destination state is still on you before pickup.

Frequently Asked

Animal Transport questions, answered straight.

What animals can you transport?
Dogs, cats, and most household pets are the most common jobs. Livestock and larger animals are handled case by case. Tell us the species, size, and distance, and we’ll tell you honestly whether it’s something we can run ourselves or need to bring in a specialized hauler for.
How do you keep the animal safe and comfortable?
Trips are planned with rest, water, and bathroom stops built in, not just point A to point B on a timer. Crates, restraints, or carriers are expected. We’ll tell you what to provide.
Do I need any paperwork to move my pet across state lines?
Sometimes, depending on the species and states involved. We’ll ask the right questions when you request a quote and flag anything you need to line up before pickup.
Can I get updates during the trip?
Yes, check-ins during longer trips are standard, especially for animals traveling without their owner.
What if my animal has medical needs or takes medication?
Tell us in the job details. It changes trip planning (stop timing, temperature, handling), and we’d rather know upfront than find out on the road.
