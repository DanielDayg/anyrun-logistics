Number of Stops

Good to Know

Legal & Regulatory Note

AnyRun delivers items and messages, not legal notices requiring formal service of process. If you need something legally served, that requires a licensed process server, not a courier. Ask us and we’ll point you in the right direction.

Frequently Asked

Courier & Messages questions, answered straight.

What can you deliver?
Documents, packages, gifts, keys, sealed envelopes, anything legal that fits in a vehicle and needs a real person to hand it off. If you’re not sure something qualifies, ask. Most things do.
How fast can something be delivered?
Same-day is often possible for local and regional jobs, depending on distance and when you book. Tell us your deadline and we’ll tell you honestly whether it’s realistic.
Can you deliver something in person and confirm it was received?
Yes. Proof of delivery, whether that’s a signature, a photo, or a call once it’s handed off, is something you can ask for on any job.
Is this the same as using a courier app or rideshare delivery?
No. You’re working directly with the person doing the job, not a rotating pool of gig drivers. The same person you talk to when booking is accountable for the delivery.
Can you deliver a message, not just a physical item?
Yes, that’s one of our more unusual but common requests. Someone needs a message delivered in person rather than by phone, text, or email. We treat it like any other job: tell us what needs to be said and to whom.
