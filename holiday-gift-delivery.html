Frequently Asked

Questions, answered straight.

How late can I book and still make it by the holiday?
It depends on distance and how booked up that week is. Ask early, holiday season fills up fast, but we'll always tell you honestly if a date is still doable.
Can you deliver to multiple houses in one trip?
Often yes, especially if they're reasonably close together. Tell us all the stops and we'll quote it as one route where it makes sense.
Can you set it up as a surprise, like leaving it under the tree?
Yes, tell us the scene you're picturing and we'll coordinate with whoever needs to let us in or be in on it.
What if the gift is fragile or oversized?
Tell us what it is and we'll handle it accordingly, that's true for holiday jobs same as any other delivery.
Do you charge more during the holidays?
Rush and peak-season jobs can cost more because of demand, but we'll always give you the real number upfront before you commit.
