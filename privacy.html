Privacy Policy

What we collect when you request a quote, and what we do with it. Last updated September 2026.

What We Collect

When you fill out a quote request, we collect your name, email, phone number, pickup and drop-off locations, and the job details you give us. That's it, we don't ask for anything else through this site.

How We Use It

We use your information to quote and coordinate your job, and to contact you about it. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone outside AnyRun Logistics except the driver, courier, or carrier actually handling your job.

How It's Stored

Quote requests are stored in a secured database with access restricted to AnyRun Logistics. Submissions can be added through this website but not read back out through it, only from our own internal dashboard.

Cookies & Tracking

This site doesn't currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

Your Choices

Want your information deleted, or want to see what we have on file? Email anyrunlogistics@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.

Changes To This Policy

We'll update this page if any of the above changes.
