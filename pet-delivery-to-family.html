Frequently Asked

Questions, answered straight.

Can you time delivery for a specific moment, like a birthday?
Yes, that's one of the most common reasons people book this. Tell us the date and time you're targeting and we'll plan the route around it.
Will you handle a puppy that's never been on a road trip?
Yes. We plan extra stops and build in settling-in time for young or first-time-traveling animals.
What if my family member isn't expecting it?
Tell us the plan, we can coordinate a knock-and-surprise delivery, or a quieter handoff if that fits the moment better.
Do I need paperwork to send a pet to another state?
Sometimes, depending on the species and states involved. We'll flag anything you need to line up before pickup when you request a quote.
Can you send updates or photos during the trip?
Yes, check-ins and photos along the way are standard for pet trips, especially ones with an emotional payoff at the other end.
