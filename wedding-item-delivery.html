Frequently Asked

Questions, answered straight.

How do you make sure it arrives on time for the ceremony?
We build in extra buffer time specifically for wedding jobs and confirm the exact arrival window with you and the venue beforehand.
Can you deliver a wedding dress without damaging it?
Yes, tell us how it's packaged, garment bag, box, or otherwise, and we'll handle it accordingly, hung and protected the whole trip if that's what it needs.
Can you coordinate directly with a wedding planner or venue?
Yes, give us their contact info and we're happy to coordinate the handoff details directly with them.
What if the wedding is out of state?
That's most of what this page is for. We run these jobs nationwide, tell us the distance and timing and we'll quote it.
Can you deliver gifts or items after the wedding too?
Yes, that's a common follow-up job, thank-you gifts, things left behind, anything else that needs to move. Just tell us what's needed.
