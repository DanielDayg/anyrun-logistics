Frequently Asked

Questions, answered straight.

Can you actually represent me, or just show up?
It depends on what "represent" needs to mean for your situation. We can attend, take notes, relay information, and handle a specific defined task on your behalf, with your written authorization where needed. We're not a substitute for legal representation, if that's what you need, tell us and we'll be upfront about it.
What kind of meetings do people send you to?
Closings, property walkthroughs, family matters, business meetings, events, anything where someone needs a real person physically present and you can't be there yourself.
Can you sign documents on my behalf?
In some cases, yes, but it depends on what's being signed and what authorization is required. We'll tell you exactly what documentation we need before agreeing to the job.
How do I know what happened at the meeting?
You'll get a recap, however you want it: written notes, a phone call right after, or both.
Is this legal representation?
No. We're not attorneys and this isn't legal representation. If your situation needs that, tell us and we'll say so plainly instead of taking the job.
