Frequently Asked

Questions, answered straight.

Will you get everything on my list even if the other person doesn't want to hand it over?
We'll do our best to retrieve what's agreed to be yours, but we're not able to force a handoff or get involved in a dispute. If something goes wrong, we'll tell you exactly what happened.
Do I need to tell the other person we're coming?
Generally yes, an unexpected stranger asking for your belongings tends to go worse than a coordinated pickup. We can advise on how to set it up smoothly.
Is this like a civil standby with police?
No, we're not law enforcement and can't provide that kind of legal backup. If your situation needs a police-accompanied retrieval, that's a different service, tell us and we'll be upfront about it.
Can you pick up from a storage unit or an ex's new address?
Yes, tell us the location and any access details, codes, keys, timing, and we'll plan around it.
How private is this?
Completely. We don't discuss your situation with anyone outside AnyRun Logistics.
